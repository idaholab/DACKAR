# **Condition Report (CR) - Elevated Vibration on AFW Pump P-101A**

| Field    | Value                       | Field          | Value              |
|----------|-----------------------------|----------------|--------------------|
| CR ID    | CR-2026-00123               | Date Initiated | 2026-02-06         |
| System   | AFW                         | Equipment      | P-101A             |
| Location | Turbine Building Elev. 468' | Initiator      | Operator (Example) |

#### **1. Condition Description**

During routine surveillance, AFW Pump P-101A exhibited elevated vibration after start. Vibration channel VB-101A peaked at 6.2 mils (alarm setpoint 5.0 mils) with stable flow at 465 gpm and discharge pressure at 1175 psig. No bearing temperature alarms were observed.

#### **2. Immediate Actions**

Operator stopped P-101A within 2 minutes of alarm. Control Room notified. Train A declared inoperable pending evaluation. Condition entered into the corrective action program.

#### **3. Operability / Safety Significance**

AFW Train B remained available. No safety injection or reactor trip occurred. This CR is categorized as low safety significance for training/test purposes.

### **4. Apparent Cause (if known)**

Unknown at initiation. Potential contributors include reduced suction pressure margin and coupling misalignment.
