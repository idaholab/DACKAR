panels.candidates¶

Candidates panel: ranked causality candidates and the pre-refine delta.

Renders the causality_candidates artifact (retained/filtered counts, a per-candidate score-breakdown chart, and per-candidate detail) and, when a pre-refine snapshot is available, a v1 to v2 ranking-delta table.
