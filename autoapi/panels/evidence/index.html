panels.evidence¶

Evidence panel: retrieval health, doc-type mix, and per-candidate excerpts.

Reads the evidence_bundle artifact (results, provenance, retrieval scope, planned queries, candidate-evidence summary) and the causality_candidates artifact to order and label hits. Honors the rca_viz_evidence_filter session value so jumps from the Candidates/RCA Card tabs land pre-filtered.
