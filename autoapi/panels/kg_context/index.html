panels.kg_context¶

KG Context panel: component seeding, subgraph topology, failure modes, events.

Reads the kg_context artifact to render a seed-match bar chart, an interactive Pyvis subgraph (asset, components, failure modes, past events, upstream paths), and filterable tables of failure modes, in-scope documents (with extracted causal statements), and a past-event timeline.
