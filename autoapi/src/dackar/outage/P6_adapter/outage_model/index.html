Transform a real Primavera P6 XER export into the canonical outage dataset.

The transformer reads common P6 XER tables directly, including: PROJECT, PROJWBS, TASK, TASKPRED, RSRC, TASKRSRC, CALENDAR, ACTVCODE, TASKACTV, and ACTVTYPE when present.
