src.dackar.outage.outage_uncertainty.visualization.plots¶

outage_uncertainty.visualization.plots — Reusable plot functions for outage uncertainty analysis.

All functions follow a consistent signature:

plot_*(data, …, *, title=None, ax=None or figsize=None) -> (fig, axes)

They can be used standalone (fig created internally) or composed into a larger figure by passing an existing axes object.

src.dackar.outage.outage_uncertainty.visualization.plots.plot_routine_vs_disruption(dist, activity_id='', *, planned_hours=None, title=None, figsize=None)[source]¶

Two-panel figure visualising routine vs. disruption-driven execution modes.

This corresponds to Slide 6 step 3: ‘Separate routine execution (typical cases) from disruption-driven outliers (scope expansions, rework, parts delays).’

Left panel

Overlaid histograms — routine pool (blue) and disruption-driven pool (red), with vertical marker lines for the planned baseline, routine P50, routine P80, and mixture-aware P80. The IQR separation fence is annotated when available.

Right panel

Empirical CDF curves comparing the routine-only distribution against the full mixture (sampled from both pools with mixture_weight probability for the disruption mode). The gap between the two P80 lines is the contingency underestimate when disruption is ignored.

Parameters:

  • dist (DurationDistribution with samples, optionally) – extended_samples and mixture_weight.

  • activity_id (Label shown in the title.)

  • planned_hours (Baseline planned duration to mark as a vertical line.)

  • title (str | None)

  • figsize (tuple | None)

Return type:

tuple
