src.dackar.outage.policy¶

Shared regulatory / LCO decision policy for the outage pipeline.

Stage F (option feasibility) and Stage G (decision status) both reason about the LCO action-level clock and regulatory constraints. Previously each stage kept its own copy of the relevant LCO-status sets, and the two drifted — the cause of the unknown → DEFER gap (an undetermined clock was treated as safe in Stage F but unsafe in Stage G). This module is the single source of truth for that policy so the two stages cannot disagree.

LCO clock status vocabulary (produced by Stage A lco_clock_status):

not_applicable no LCO in effect. normal clock running, deadline beyond the current shift (known). urgent clock running, action needed this shift. critical clock running, immediate action required. expired action-level deadline already passed. unknown clock running but the deadline was NOT supplied.

Two distinct policy questions, two distinct sets:

  • LCO_ACTIVE_STATUSES — the clock is actively running, so deferring the work out of this outage is NOT feasible. Includes normal (a known future deadline still forbids deferral) and unknown (running clock, treat as unsafe).

  • LCO_FAIL_CLOSED_STATUSES — the decision must fail closed: no PROCEED or MONITOR, escalate for review instead. Excludes normal (a known, non-imminent deadline does not by itself force escalation) but includes unknown (an undetermined deadline is treated as unsafe).

unknown is deliberately in BOTH: a running clock with no supplied deadline is the conservative/unsafe case for either question.

src.dackar.outage.policy.is_fail_closed(intake_result, schedule_impact=None)[source]¶

True when the decision must fail closed (escalate, never PROCEED/MONITOR).

Fail-closed conditions:

  • the LCO action-level clock is in a fail-closed status (expired/critical/urgent/unknown), OR

  • any regulatory constraint is present, OR

  • critical-path impact could not be computed (Stage E set cp_impact.cp_impact_unavailable because the duration was unknown) — “couldn’t compute CP impact” must not read as “no CP impact”.
