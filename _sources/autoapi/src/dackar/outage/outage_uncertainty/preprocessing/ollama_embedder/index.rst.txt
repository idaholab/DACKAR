.. py:module:: src.dackar.outage.outage_uncertainty.preprocessing.ollama_embedder

.. autoapi-nested-parse::

   Ollama-backed text embedder for nuclear outage activity descriptions.

   The embedder and its cosine-similarity utility are the domain-agnostic
   :class:`dackar.similarity.OllamaEmbedder.OllamaEmbedder` and
   :func:`dackar.similarity.OllamaEmbedder.cosine_similarity`.  This module
   re-exports them under the names the outage pipeline uses: the embedder replaces
   the :class:`SimpleEmbedder` stub in
   :mod:`preprocessing.feature_builder` and feeds
   :class:`~outage_uncertainty.retrieval.semantic_similarity.EmbeddingSemanticScorer`.

   Embedding model recommendations (from benchmark on
   ``outage_cleaning_benchmark.csv``):

   * ``nomic-embed-text:latest`` — best discrimination ratio (1.415), fastest,
     274 MB.  Recommended default for most deployments.
   * ``mxbai-embed-large:latest`` — best Precision@5/10 on category retrieval,
     ~2.4x larger (669 MB).  Preferred when retrieval ranking quality matters most.
